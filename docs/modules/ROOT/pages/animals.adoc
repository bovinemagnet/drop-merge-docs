= Drop & Merge — Animals

Inspired by the beloved cumulative nursery rhyme "There Was an Old Lady Who Swallowed a Fly", this edition sends each new animal in to catch the last one. The pieces follow the rhyme's own escalating logic — from a tiny fly, through spider, bird, cat and dog, all the way up to the Old Lady herself.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to the cumulative nursery rhyme, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The animal ladder

You merge your way up 9 animals. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.

[cols="1,3,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Tier |Piece |Points |How you get it

|1 |Fly |1 |Dropped
|2 |Spider |3 |Dropped
|3 |Bird |6 |Dropped
|4 |Cat |10 |Dropped
|5 |Dog |15 |Dropped
|6 |Goat |21 |Merged
|7 |Cow |28 |Merged
|8 |Horse |36 |Merged
|9 |Old Lady |45 |Merged — top tier
|===

Clearing the two Old Lady pieces awards a 250-point bonus.

Tap *Characters* on the menu to browse every piece in this edition with a fact about it.
