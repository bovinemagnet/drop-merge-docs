= Drop & Merge — Australia

The Australian Aboriginal Dreamtime is one of the world's oldest living mythologies, peopled with spirit creatures that shaped the land, sky and water. This edition moves from bone wisps and gecko spirits through the fearsome Yowie and the lurking Bunyip, up to the great Rainbow Serpent — creator of the waterways — and its transcendent cosmic form.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to Australian Aboriginal Dreamtime folklore, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The Dreamtime ladder
