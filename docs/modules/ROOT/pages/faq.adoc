= FAQ

== What is Drop & Merge?

Drop & Merge is a casual drop-and-merge puzzle game: drop pieces into a jar, and two of the same kind merge into the next kind up.
See xref:overview.adoc[About the game] for a full introduction.

== Which edition should I play?

Every edition plays identically; only the theme and the ladder of pieces differ.
Pick the theme you like best and start there.
See xref:games.adoc[The games] for the full list.

== How do I play?

Drag to aim, release or tap to drop; land two of the same piece together to merge them.
See xref:gameplay.adoc[How to play] for step-by-step guidance.

== How does a run end?

When pieces settle above the danger line for about two seconds, or when the run timer reaches zero.
See xref:avoiding-game-over.adoc[Avoiding game over] for the full detail.

== I keep losing quickly — what can I do?

Merge pieces early instead of letting them pile up; shake the pile to knock a high piece down; the Anti-frustration help setting (on by default) keeps a match available; you can also lengthen the run timer.
See xref:strategy.adoc[Tips & strategy] and xref:settings.adoc[Settings].

== What is the rainbow piece?

The wildcard: it merges with whatever piece it touches, making it the perfect tool for clearing a piece you cannot otherwise pair.
See xref:wildcard.adoc[The wildcard].

== Is my score saved?

Yes. Your best score and a leaderboard of your ten best runs are stored on your device.
See xref:scoring.adoc[Scoring & chains].

== Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Drop & Merge needs no account and no sign-in, and a run can be played fully offline.
See xref:overview.adoc[About the game] and the xref:privacy.adoc[Privacy policy].

== How do I turn off the sound, or change the run length?

In Settings: Sound effects and Haptics can each be turned off, and the Run timer can be set from one to ten minutes.
See xref:settings.adoc[Settings].

== What are tilt and shake controls?

Optional motion controls on phones and tablets: tilt steers a falling piece left or right, and shake jostles the pile to knock pieces down.
See xref:controls.adoc[Controls].

== Which devices can I play on?

Android and iOS phones and tablets, web browsers, and Windows, macOS and Linux computers.
