= Drop & Merge — Mythology

A globe-spanning collection of legendary creatures drawn from the world's great mythologies. Goblins, harpies and the kappa share the lower rungs with the minotaur, Cerberus and Fenrir; higher up you will find the Hydra, the Kraken, Orochi and the Dragon Turtle before the ladder culminates in the awe-inspiring Celestial God Beast.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to world mythologies, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The mythology ladder

You merge your way up 16 legendary creatures. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.
