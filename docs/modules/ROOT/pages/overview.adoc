= About the game

*Drop & Merge* is a casual physics puzzle game. You drop themed pieces into a jar; matching pieces merge into bigger ones; bigger pieces score more. It is easy to pick up and quick to play — a good fit for short sessions and high-score chasing alike.

== One game, many editions

Drop & Merge is published as a *family of separate apps*. Each edition has its own theme and its own ladder of pieces, but the rules, controls and scoring are identical across all of them. See xref:games.adoc[The games] for the full list — fruit, world mythologies, the Chinese zodiac, K-pop idols and more.

== Who it is for

Drop & Merge suits players who enjoy:

* short, repeatable puzzle sessions;
* steady, satisfying progress up a ladder;
* chasing a personal best score.

== Playing offline and privately

Drop & Merge needs no account and no sign-in, and you can play a full run with no network connection. Your best scores, leaderboard and settings are stored on your device. See the xref:privacy.adoc[Privacy policy] for how information is handled.

== Platforms

The game runs on Android and iOS phones and tablets, in a web browser, and on Windows, macOS and Linux desktops. Tilt and shake use device sensors and so are available on phones and tablets; on a computer the same actions are on-screen buttons.
