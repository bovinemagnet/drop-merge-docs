= Drop & Merge — Japan

The supernatural world of Japanese folklore is packed with spirits, shape-shifters and legendary beings. This edition climbs from restless haunted skulls through famous yokai — the mischievous kappa, the tanuki, the nine-tailed kitsune, the fearsome oni and the winged tengu — up through legendary deities to the all-powerful Yokai Emperor.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to Japanese folklore, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The yokai ladder

You merge your way up 16 yokai. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.

[cols="1,3,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Tier |Piece |Points |How you get it

|1 |Haunted Skull |1 |Dropped
|2 |Kodama |3 |Dropped
|3 |Slime Spirit |6 |Dropped
|4 |Kappa |10 |Dropped
|5 |Tanuki |15 |Dropped
|6 |Kitsune |21 |Merged
|7 |Oni |28 |Merged
|8 |Tengu |36 |Merged
|9 |Nekomata |45 |Merged
|10 |Rokurokubi |55 |Merged
|11 |Jorogumo |66 |Merged
|12 |Yuki-onna |78 |Merged
|13 |Orochi |91 |Merged
|14 |Raijin |105 |Merged
|15 |Amaterasu Avatar |120 |Merged
|16 |Yokai Emperor |136 |Merged — top tier
|===

Clearing the two Yokai Emperor pieces awards a 700-point bonus.

Tap *Characters* on the menu to browse every piece in this edition with a fact about it.
