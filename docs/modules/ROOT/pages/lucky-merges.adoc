= Lucky merges

A lucky merge is a rare bonus that lets you merge two matching pieces without a physical collision.

== How it works

With a small chance on each drop, the game offers you a lucky merge.
A banner appears across the HUD: *"Lucky merge ready -- tap two of the same kind"*.
While the offer is active:

. Tap any piece on the board to select it as your first target.
. Tap a second piece of the *same tier* to complete the merge -- the two pieces merge into the next tier up, just as if they had collided normally.

You can tap the *X* on the banner at any time to cancel the offer and return to normal play.

== Rules and edge cases

* You cannot select a wildcard piece or a piece at the very top of the ladder (there is no tier above it to merge into).
* If you tap two pieces of *different* tiers your first selection is cleared so you can try again -- the offer itself stays active.
* The offer stays until you either resolve it or cancel it, even across several drops.

== On the game-over screen

Lucky merges are counted separately and shown on the game-over screen when you completed at least one during the run.

== Tips

Save the lucky merge for a piece that is trapped or hard to reach -- it does not matter where on the board the two pieces are, so you can pair two pieces at opposite ends of the jar.
If the current state of the board gives you no obvious pair, drop a neutral piece first to open up a match.
