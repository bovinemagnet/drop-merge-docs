= Power-ups

Power-ups are special abilities you earn during a run by building long merge chains.
They appear on the *ability rail* above the piece ladder in the HUD, each with a charge count.
Tap a button to spend one charge.

== How you earn them

Each power-up has a chain milestone.
The first time your chain reaches that depth in a run, you gain a charge.
Chaining longer in the same run does not re-award the same threshold -- each milestone fires once per run.

== The four power-ups

*Upgrade.*
Bumps your currently held piece up one tier on the ladder.
Use it just before a drop to turn an awkward small piece into something more useful.
You can hold up to two Upgrade charges at once.
Upgrade is awarded when your chain first reaches depth 3.

*Swap.*
Drops your held piece down one tier.
Handy when the piece you're holding is too large to fit a gap you're trying to fill.
You can hold up to two Swap charges at once.
Swap is awarded when your chain first reaches depth 2.

*Compact.*
Delivers a firm inward and downward nudge to every piece on the board, squeezing the pile into its own gaps.
Use it when the pile is messy and uneven to buy a bit of headroom.
You can hold one Compact charge at a time.
Compact is awarded when your chain first reaches depth 4.

*Bomb.*
Removes a single piece from the board without a merge.
Tap *Bomb* to arm it -- the button glows to show it's ready -- then tap any piece on the board to remove it.
If you miss the piece, the bomb stays armed so you can try again.
The charge is only spent when a piece is actually removed.
You can hold one Bomb charge at a time.
Bomb is awarded when your chain first reaches depth 5.

NOTE: Which power-ups are available and how many charges you can carry depends on the edition.
Some packs may ship fewer power-up types or different charge caps.
