= Strategy

== Beginner Tips

* Keep the center or highest-traffic area clear.
* Merge early to avoid clutter.
* Do not overcommit to one setup if board space is shrinking.

== Intermediate Tips

* Plan at least two turns ahead.
* Build near-compatible clusters that can convert quickly.
* Leave room for unexpected piece sequences.

== Common Mistakes

* Filling edges too early with low-value pieces
* Chasing one large merge while ignoring board pressure
* Ignoring chain opportunities in favor of immediate small points

For fundamentals, review xref:gameplay.adoc[Gameplay] and xref:scoring.adoc[Scoring].
