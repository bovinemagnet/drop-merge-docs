= Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the *Drop and Merge* game ("the game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play.
By installing or playing Drop and Merge, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Last updated: 19 May 2026

== Information We Collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other personal details to play the game.

=== Information Collected Automatically

When you play, the game and the third-party services it uses may collect:

* *Device and identifier information* -- device model, operating system version, language, and a resettable advertising identifier (such as the Google Advertising ID or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers).
* *Usage and gameplay data* -- events such as levels played, scores, session length, and feature interactions, used to understand how the game is played.
* *Approximate location* -- a general region (for example, your country) derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.
* *Diagnostics and crash data* -- technical information about errors and performance, used to diagnose and fix problems.

=== Information You Provide

If you contact us for support, we receive whatever information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry.

== How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

* Operate, maintain, and improve the game.
* Understand how players use the game and guide future updates.
* Display advertising that helps keep the game free to play.
* Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes, bugs, and performance issues.
* Respond to support requests and other enquiries.
* Protect against fraud, abuse, and misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

== Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information for money.
We share information only as follows:

* *With service providers* -- the advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting partners described above, so they can perform services for us.
* *For legal reasons* -- when required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of players, the public, or us.
* *In a business transfer* -- if the game or its developer is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

== Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.
Advertising and analytics data is retained according to each third-party provider's own retention schedule.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

== Your Privacy Rights

=== European Economic Area and United Kingdom (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, as well as the right to data portability.
Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

We process data on the following lawful bases:

* *Legitimate interests* -- operating, securing, and improving the game, and showing non-personalised ads.
* *Consent* -- showing personalised ads and any other processing that requires consent, which you can manage through your device settings or the in-game consent prompt.
* *Legal obligation* -- where processing is required to comply with the law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

=== California (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and shared, the right to request deletion, the right to correct inaccurate information, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.
Sharing the advertising identifier with advertising partners for personalised advertising may be considered "sharing" under California law; you can opt out by limiting ad personalisation in your device settings as described above.
We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

== Children's Privacy

Drop and Merge is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

== International Data Transfers

The game and its third-party providers may process information in countries other than the one in which you live.
Where information is transferred internationally, we and our providers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect it.
