= Drop & Merge — Fruit

Start with a single cherry and merge upward through the everyday fruits you know — strawberries, grapes, oranges, apples — before reaching the exotic and the enormous. The ladder ends with a satisfying giant watermelon that takes real skill to produce.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to fruit, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The fruit ladder

You merge your way up 11 fruits. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.

|===
|Tier |Piece |Description |Points |How you get it

|1 |image:pieces/fruit/cherry.png[Cherry,96] |*Cherry* +
Cherries are stone fruits, so each one has a hard pit surrounded by sweet flesh. |1 |Dropped
|2 |image:pieces/fruit/strawberry.png[Strawberry,96] |*Strawberry* +
A strawberry wears its seeds on the outside, with tiny achenes dotted across the surface. |3 |Dropped
|3 |image:pieces/fruit/grape.png[Grape,96] |*Grape* +
Grapes grow in clusters on vines that can live and produce fruit for decades. |6 |Dropped
|4 |image:pieces/fruit/green_grapes.png[Green Grapes,96] |*Green Grapes* +
Green grapes are often green because they make less of the red and purple anthocyanin pigments. |10 |Dropped
|5 |image:pieces/fruit/orange.png[Orange,96] |*Orange* +
Oranges are hesperidia, a type of berry with a leathery rind and juicy segments. |15 |Dropped
|6 |image:pieces/fruit/apple.png[Apple,96] |*Apple* +
Apple trees are usually grafted because seeds do not grow into exact copies of the parent fruit. |21 |Merged
|7 |image:pieces/fruit/peach.png[Peach,96] |*Peach* +
Peaches are related to almonds, cherries, plums, and apricots. |28 |Merged
|8 |image:pieces/fruit/coconut.png[Coconut,96] |*Coconut* +
A coconut is a fibrous drupe, not a true nut in the everyday botanical sense. |36 |Merged
|9 |image:pieces/fruit/dragon_fruit.png[Dragon Fruit,96] |*Dragon Fruit* +
Dragon fruit grows on climbing cactus plants rather than on trees. |45 |Merged
|10 |image:pieces/fruit/pineapple.png[Pineapple,96] |*Pineapple* +
A pineapple is a multiple fruit, formed from many flowers fused together. |55 |Merged

Watermelons are over 90 percent water, which is why they feel so refreshing. |66 |Merged — top tier
|===

Clearing the two Watermelon pieces awards a 300-point bonus.

Tap *Characters* on the menu to browse every piece in this edition with a fact about it.
