= Drop & Merge — Greece

Classical Greek mythology brings you a ladder of legendary beings — from bleached skeleton warriors through famous monsters such as the Minotaur, Cyclops, Medusa, the winged Pegasus and the many-headed Hydra, before ascending to the Titans and the great Olympian gods themselves. Every piece carries a story from the ancient world.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to Greek mythology, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The Greek ladder

You merge your way up 16 Greek myths. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.

[cols="1,3,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Tier |Piece |Points |How you get it

|1 |Skull |1 |Dropped
|2 |Skeleton Warrior |3 |Dropped
|3 |Harpy |6 |Dropped
|4 |Satyr |10 |Dropped
|5 |Minotaur |15 |Dropped
|6 |Cyclops |21 |Merged
|7 |Cerberus |28 |Merged
|8 |Medusa |36 |Merged
|9 |Pegasus |45 |Merged
|10 |Hydra |55 |Merged
|11 |Chimera |66 |Merged
|12 |Kraken |78 |Merged
|13 |Titan |91 |Merged
|14 |Prometheus |105 |Merged
|15 |Hades |120 |Merged
|16 |Zeus Ascended |136 |Merged — top tier
|===

Clearing the two Zeus Ascended pieces awards a 700-point bonus.

Tap *Characters* on the menu to browse every piece in this edition with a fact about it.
