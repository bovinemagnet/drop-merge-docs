= Drop & Merge — Indonesia

Indonesian island folklore is a vivid tapestry of spirits, demons and divine guardians drawn from Balinese and Javanese traditions. This edition journeys from humble skull spirits and jungle imps through the paired adversaries Barong and Rangda, the mighty monkey warrior Hanoman and the great sea dragon, all the way to the majestic Cosmic Garuda who rules the sky.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to Indonesian island folklore, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The island-spirit ladder

You merge your way up 16 island spirits. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.
