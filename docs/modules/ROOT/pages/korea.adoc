= Drop & Merge — Korea

Korean folklore brims with mischievous goblins, shape-shifting foxes and iron-devouring monsters drawn from centuries of myth and legend. This edition climbs from spirit embers and mountain toads through the club-swinging dokkaebi, the nine-tailed gumiho and the iron-eating Bulgasari, all the way to the three-legged Samjoko, the great Imugi serpent and, at the summit, the Eternal Sky Dragon.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to Korean folklore, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The folklore ladder

You merge your way up 16 folklore spirits. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.
