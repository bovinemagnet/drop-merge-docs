= Drop & Merge — Zodiac

The twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac appear in their traditional order — Rat, Ox, Tiger and all the rest through to Pig — making this the most compact edition in the Drop & Merge family. Every piece is instantly familiar, and the short twelve-tier ladder gives the game a brisk, accessible pace.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to the Chinese zodiac, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The zodiac ladder

You merge your way up 12 zodiac animals. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.
