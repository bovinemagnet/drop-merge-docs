= Gameplay

== Basic Concept

In each turn, the player places a piece on the board.
When matching pieces connect according to game rules, they merge into a higher-value piece.

== Dropping Pieces

Typical turn flow:

. The next piece is shown to the player.
. The player chooses where to drop it.
. The board updates and resolves merges.
