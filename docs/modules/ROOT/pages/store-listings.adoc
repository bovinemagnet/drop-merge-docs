= Store listings

Submission copy for the App Store and Google Play. Each edition of Drop & Merge is published as a separate app with its own listing — copy the three blocks for the edition you are submitting.

[NOTE]
====
Google Play allows up to 30 characters for the app title, 80 for the short description and 4,000 for the full description. The App Store allows up to 30 characters for the app name and subtitle and 4,000 for the description. Where a store's limit is shorter than the text below, trim to fit.
====

== Drop & Merge — Fruit

*App name*

[listing]
----
Drop & Merge - Fruit
----

*Short description*

[listing]
----
Drop and merge fruit — cherries to the giant watermelon. Easy to play!
----

*Full description*

[listing]
----
Drop, merge, and climb your way to the giant watermelon!

Drop & Merge - Fruit is a relaxing physics puzzle game. Drop fruit into the jar, and when two of the same fruit touch they merge into the next fruit up — cherries become strawberries, strawberries become grapes, all the way to the mighty watermelon.

It is easy to learn and hard to put down. Line up quick merges to build score-multiplying chains, catch the rare rainbow wildcard that merges with anything, and keep the pile below the danger line for as long as you can.

FEATURES
• Simple one-handed play — just aim and drop
• Merge 11 fruit, from cherry to watermelon
• Chain your merges for big score multipliers
• A rare rainbow wildcard that merges with anything
• Beat your best score and a local top-ten leaderboard
• An adjustable run timer, from 1 to 10 minutes
• Optional tilt and shake controls on phones and tablets
• Orchard and Twilight themes
• No account and no sign-in — play offline, anywhere

Drop & Merge - Fruit is part of the Drop & Merge family of games. Same addictive gameplay, many themes — try the mythology, zodiac and K-pop editions too.

Can you reach the watermelon?
