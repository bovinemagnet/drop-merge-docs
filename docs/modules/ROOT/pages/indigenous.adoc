= Drop & Merge — Indigenous

The spirit creatures of North American Indigenous folklore fill this edition, taking you from the small animals of the prairie through powerful guardian beings — the Wendigo, the Buffalo Guardian and the Water Panther — all the way to the great World Thunderbird whose wings span the sky. Each piece reflects stories and traditions from the Indigenous peoples of North America.

This is one edition of *Drop & Merge*. Its pieces are themed to Indigenous North American folklore, but it plays exactly like every other edition — see xref:games.adoc[The games] for the rest of the family.

== The spirit ladder

You merge your way up 15 spirit creatures. You can drop only the five smallest; every larger piece is made by merging two of the kind below it.
