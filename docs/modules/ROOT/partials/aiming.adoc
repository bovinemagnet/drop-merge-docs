// Shared partial: aiming and dropping a piece.
Your current piece waits at the top of the jar.
Drag your finger -- or move the mouse -- left and right and the piece follows, always staying inside the walls.
A faint dashed guide line shows where it will land.
On *Easy* difficulty a translucent ghost of the piece also appears at the predicted landing spot, so you can see exactly where it will sit before you commit.
See xref:settings.adoc[] to change the difficulty.

* Release the drag, or simply tap, to drop the piece straight down.
* A short cooldown of about half a second follows each drop before the next piece is ready, so there is no need to rush.
* The *NEXT* panel previews the piece coming after this one, so you can plan two pieces ahead.

Pieces fall under gravity, bounce a little and settle into the pile.
You only ever choose _where_ a piece enters the jar -- physics does the rest.
