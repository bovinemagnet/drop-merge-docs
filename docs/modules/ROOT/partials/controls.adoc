// Shared partial: the control schemes.
*Touch and mouse* -- drag to aim, then release or tap to drop. This works on every platform.

*Tilt steering* -- on phones and tablets you can switch on *Tilt controls* in Settings.
Tilting the device left or right then nudges a piece while it is still falling; it never moves the settled pile.
Tilt is off by default.

*Shake* -- give the phone a shake to jostle the pile.
It is useful for knocking a piece down off the danger line.
There is a short cooldown of a couple of seconds between shakes.

*On a computer or in a browser* -- there is no tilt sensor, so the in-game display shows buttons instead: tilt left, tilt right and shuffle.

*Pause* -- the pause button stops play and freezes the timer until you resume.
