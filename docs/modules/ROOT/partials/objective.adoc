// Shared partial: the goal of every Drop & Merge game.
Every Drop & Merge game works the same way.
You drop pieces into the jar, and whenever two of the *same* piece touch they merge into the next, larger piece.
Larger pieces are worth far more points, so the aim is to keep merging up the ladder while the jar slowly fills.

A run continues until one of two things happens:

* pieces pile up past the *danger line* near the top of the jar, or
* the optional run timer reaches zero.

When a run ends your score is saved. The goal is simple: beat your best.
