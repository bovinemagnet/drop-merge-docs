// Shared partial: scoring and chain multipliers.
Every merge scores points equal to the value of the piece it creates.
Values rise steeply up the ladder, so reaching the larger pieces matters far more than making many small merges.
Each game's page lists the exact value of every piece.

*Chains.*
Merges that follow one another quickly -- each within about 0.6 seconds of the last -- build a chain, and a multiplier is applied to the points:

[cols="1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Chain step |Score multiplier

|First merge |×1
|Second merge |×1.5
|Third merge |×2
|Fourth merge and beyond |×3
|===

Pause for longer than about 0.6 seconds and the chain resets to ×1.
Setting up cascades -- one drop that triggers merge after merge -- is the key to a high score.

*Top-of-the-ladder bonus.*
Clearing the two biggest pieces awards a large bonus on top of the merge score, and that bonus is multiplied by any active chain.

Your *best score* is saved on your device and shown on the menu.
