= On your phone or tablet

Drop & Merge was built for a phone first, and it plays the same way on Android and on iOS.
These pages cover the parts that are specific to each platform: installing the app, the motion controls, what the app stores on your device, and what to try when something is not behaving.

== The same on both

The game itself does not change between platforms.
The board, the pieces, the scoring, the power-ups and the Daily Challenge are identical, and so is every option on the Settings screen.

== What differs

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| |Android |iPhone & iPad

|Minimum version
|Android 7.0 (Nougat)
|iOS 13

|Permissions
|None at all — the motion sensor needs no permission on Android
|iOS may ask once for motion access when you first use tilt

|Removing the app without losing scores
|Not available — uninstalling clears the data
|*Offload App* keeps your data; *Delete App* does not

|Carrying scores to a new device
|Possible through Google's backup for apps
|Possible through an iCloud or computer backup
|===

Both are covered in full on the platform pages.

== Playing somewhere else

Drop & Merge also runs in a web browser and on Windows, macOS and Linux computers.
Those have no motion sensor, so the game shows on-screen tilt and shuffle buttons instead — see xref:ROOT:controls.adoc[Controls].
