= Playing on Android

Drop & Merge runs on phones and tablets running Android.
This page covers everything specific to the Android edition, from installing it to sorting out a device quirk.

== What you need

Drop & Merge needs Android 7.0 (Nougat) or later.

include::partial$requirements.adoc[]

== Installing

Search for "Drop & Merge" in the Google Play Store.
Tap *Install*, wait for the download to finish, then open the app from the store or from your home screen.

== Tilt and shake

include::partial$motion-controls.adoc[]

Android does not ask for a permission before either control works.
The motion sensor they both read needs no runtime permission on this platform, so no prompt ever appears.

== Permissions

Drop & Merge asks for nothing at all.
There is no internet permission, no location, no advertising ID and nothing else -- so there is nothing for you to grant.

You can check this for yourself on your own device.
Open *Settings > Apps > Drop & Merge > Permissions* and you will find the list empty.

== If something is not working

Most of the fixes below start in the game's own Settings screen.

.The Settings screen
image::android/settings.png[The Drop & Merge settings screen on Android,width=280]

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Symptom |What to try

|No sound
|Check your device volume and Do Not Disturb, then check the *Sound effects* setting in xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings].

|No vibration
|Check the *Haptics* setting in xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings], and your device's own system-wide vibration or touch-feedback toggle.

|Tilt doing nothing
|*Tilt controls* is off by default. Turn it on in xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings].

|Shake doing nothing
|Give the device a brisk shake rather than a gentle one, and remember there is a short cooldown of just under a second between shakes.

|Game feels slow or stuttery
|Battery saver mode can throttle the device; try closing other apps. The *Reduce motion* setting in xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings] calms the visual effects too.

|Running out of storage
|The *Collection* edition is the biggest download, at roughly 100 MB, because it carries every theme. A single-theme edition such as Fruit is a good deal smaller.
|===

== Your scores and data

Here is what Drop & Merge keeps on your device, and how to clear it.

include::partial$data.adoc[]

If you have Google's backup for apps turned on, your saved data can be carried over automatically when you set up a new phone and reinstall the app -- this depends on your Google backup settings and Android version, so treat it as a possibility rather than a promise.

Android's own *Clear data* (or *Clear storage*) option, found under *Settings > Apps > Drop & Merge > Storage*, goes further than the in-game *Reset progress* button.
As well as your best score and leaderboard, it also clears your Daily Challenge scores and streak, your unlocked themes and your settings.
