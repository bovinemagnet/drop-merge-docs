= Playing on iPhone and iPad

Drop & Merge plays as a small, self-contained app on iPhone and iPad.
This page covers what you need, how to install it, and what to try if something is not behaving.

== What you need

Drop & Merge needs iOS 13 or later, on an iPhone or iPad.

include::partial$requirements.adoc[]

== Installing

Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad and search for *Drop & Merge*.
Each themed edition -- Fruit, Animals, Collection and the rest -- is a separate app with its own icon, so look for the edition you want and tap *Get* to install it.

NOTE: Drop & Merge is not yet published on the App Store. Once the listing goes live, a direct link will be added here.

== Tilt and shake

include::partial$motion-controls.adoc[]

The first time you use tilt, iOS may show a permission prompt for Motion & Fitness, since tilt steering reads the device's motion sensor.
If you see it, tap *Allow*.
If tilt still does not seem to respond, open the iOS *Settings* app (not the game's own Settings) and check *Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness*, then check Drop & Merge's own entry further down the Settings app.

== If something is not working

Most of the fixes below start in the game's own Settings screen.

.The Settings screen
image::ios/settings.png[The Drop & Merge settings screen on iPhone,width=280]

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Symptom |What to try

|No sound
|Check your device's volume, and check the Ring/Silent switch or any Focus mode is not muting alerts.
Then check *Sound effects* is switched on in xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings].

|No vibration
|Check *Haptics* is switched on in the game's xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings].
Then check iOS *Settings > Sounds & Haptics > System Haptics* is switched on.

|Tilt does nothing
|*Tilt controls* is off by default -- turn it on in xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings] first.
If it is already on and still nothing happens, check the motion permission described above.

|Shake does nothing
|Shake needs a brisk shake, not a gentle wobble -- a gentle movement is ignored on purpose.
There is also a short cooldown of just under a second between shakes, so wait a moment and try again.

|The game feels slow
|Turn off Low Power Mode, and close any other apps running in the background.
Turning on *Reduce motion* in the game's xref:ROOT:settings.adoc[Settings] also lightens the amount of decoration on screen.

|Running low on storage
|The *Collection* edition is the largest download, at roughly 100 MB, because it bundles the artwork and audio for every theme.
A single-theme edition is a good deal smaller.
Offloading the app rather than deleting it can free up space while keeping your data -- see below.
|===

== Your scores and data

include::partial$data.adoc[]

Because the app's data lives in the space iOS sets aside for it, it is included in a normal iPhone or iPad backup, whether that is an iCloud backup or a backup to a computer.
Restoring a backup to a new device can bring your scores and settings along with the app, though this is not guaranteed for every backup and restore combination.

iOS also distinguishes between *offloading* an app and *deleting* it, and the difference matters here.
*Offload App* removes the app itself but keeps its data, so reinstalling later restores your scores and settings.
*Delete App* removes both the app and its data together, matching the description above.
